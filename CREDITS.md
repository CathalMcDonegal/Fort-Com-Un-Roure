# Fonts, exercise data and image credits

## Free Exercise DB
The illustrated exercise catalogue and exercise photographs in `exercises.json` are adapted from **Free Exercise DB**, maintained by the open-source community.

- Source repository: https://github.com/Shreyaan/free-exercise-db
- Upstream project: https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db
- Dataset: 350+ exercises selected for home training equipment and bodyweight work.
- License: Unlicense / public domain dedication, as stated by the dataset repository.
- Images are loaded from the dataset's public GitHub raw assets and cached by the app after first use.

The application interface, Catalan navigation, workout plans, progress tracking and PWA implementation are developed for Fort Com Un Roure.
